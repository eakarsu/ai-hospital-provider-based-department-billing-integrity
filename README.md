# Hospital Provider-Based Department Billing Integrity

Validate provider-based eligibility and recover compliant facility revenue while preventing modifier and notice failures.

**Primary buyer:** Hospital systems with provider-based departments. **Evidence:** department attestations, ownership, licensure, campus distance, signage, patient notices, professional claims, facility claims, modifiers, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Department eligibility registry
- Ownership licensure evidence
- Campus-distance validation
- Public awareness evidence
- Patient notice control
- Professional facility claim linking
- Place-of-service validation
- PO modifier control
- PN modifier control
- Site-neutral payment calculation
- Split-billing validation
- Charge capture review
- Corrected claim workflow
- Payment reconciliation
- Department economics
